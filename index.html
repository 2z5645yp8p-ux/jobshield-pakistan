Safety workflow

Three steps before you trust an offer

Use more than one independent source. Scammers may copy real company names, licence numbers or logos.

STEP 01

Verify the recruiter

Find the agency through BEOE’s official site. Compare the licence number, agency name, address and current status. Contact the authority if anything differs.

STEP 02

Verify the specific job

Check whether the permission / demand exists where applicable. Match the employer, job title, destination, salary and other details. A real licence does not prove every offer is real.

STEP 03

Pause before paying

Be cautious of guaranteed visas, pressure to pay immediately, personal-account transfers, unreceipted cash, and interviews conducted only through informal messaging apps.

Frequently asked questions

Know what this tool can and cannot do

Does JobShield automatically verify a licence?

No. This version provides a checklist and official links. It does not query BEOE or any government database in real time. Check the record directly with the official source.

Does “Unable to verify” mean fraud?

No. It means the information available to you is insufficient or could not be confirmed. Contact the relevant authority and do not pay until the concern is resolved.

Can a real company name be used in a scam?

Yes. Fraudsters may impersonate legitimate companies or recruiters. Find the employer’s contact details independently and confirm the offer directly.

What if I suspect a scam?

Save messages, receipts, offer letters and account details securely. Avoid publishing sensitive personal data. Contact the relevant official authority or law-enforcement channel for advice.
